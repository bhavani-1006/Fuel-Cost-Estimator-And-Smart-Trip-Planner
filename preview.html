PACK YOUR PLANS BEFORE YOU GO

A little planning goes a long way

Before you start your journey, check the route, review your vehicle’s mileage and keep an approximate fuel budget in mind. TripWise brings these details together so you can plan your travel with more confidence, whether you are visiting family, commuting, travelling for college or exploring somewhere new.

Keep some extra money aside for tolls, parking, traffic delays and unexpected detours. Real-world fuel use can change with road conditions, vehicle maintenance, passenger load and driving style, so treat every estimate as a guide rather than a guarantee.

HOW TRIPWISE HELPS

Clear route details, smarter decisions

Enter your starting point and destination, choose Car, Bus or Bike, and add your mileage and passenger count. TripWise estimates road distance, travel time, fuel needed and fuel cost using route information and an assumed fuel price. You can adjust mileage to better reflect your own vehicle.

For turn-by-turn navigation, current traffic and road updates, open the route in Google Maps. Fuel prices and travel times may change, and bus estimates represent the vehicle’s estimated fuel cost rather than the price of a passenger ticket.
